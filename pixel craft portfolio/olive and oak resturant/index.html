01 / Pull up a chair

A little story about a little kitchen.

For the meals
that turn into
memories.

Olive & Oak began with a simple thought: dinner should feel like the best part of your day. Not fussy, not hurried. Just really good food and someone you like across the table.

We follow the seasons, work with what's good, and make the sort of food that makes people ask if there's enough for one more.
